        <h2>🌍 Climate Change</h2>
        <p>
            Climate change refers to long-term changes in Earth’s temperature and

            fossil fuels and deforestation.
        </p>
        <p>
            🚨 Impacts include global warming, rising sea levels, heatwaves,

        </p>

            and supporting renewable energy.
        </p>

<!-- WASTE MANAGEMENT -->
<section class="info-box animate slide-right" id="waste">
    <div class="info-content">
        <h2>♻ Waste Management</h2>
        <p>
            Waste management involves proper collection, segregation, recycling,
            and disposal of waste to reduce pollution and protect human health.
        </p>
        <p>
            🗑 Poor waste handling leads to soil, air, and water pollution.
        </p>
        <p class="action">
            ✅ Reduce, Reuse, Recycle (3R principle) and avoid single-use plastics
            to maintain a clean campus.
        </p>
    </div>
    <img src="https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1604187351574-c75ca79f5807" alt="Waste Management">
</section>

        <p>
            Water conservation means using water efficiently to prevent water
            scarcity and ensure availability for future generations.
        </p>
        <p>
            🚿 Excessive water usage and wastage are major global concerns.
        </p>

        </p>
    </div>

        <p>

            animals, and microorganisms that maintain ecological balance.
        </p>
        <p>
            🌳 Loss of biodiversity affects food chains and ecosystems.

            initiatives.
        </p>
    </div>
